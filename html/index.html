Who are we?

Spider Club

​Spider Club is a complete social network and marketplace built on a single conviction: human connection and commerce shouldn't cost you your privacy or your safety. For years, the web forced a false choice between surveillance platforms that harvest every click and chaotic spaces with zero boundaries—we refused both. Engineered around zero data retention, Spider Club never tracks your habits, builds shadow profiles, or monetizes your personal information, yet maintains strict, zero-tolerance boundaries against violent, harmful, and unethical content. Here, you can share ideas, connect freely, and trade directly in a clean, human-first environment—a web woven not to trap you, but to protect those inside it
