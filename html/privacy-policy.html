Privacy Policy

​At Spider Club, we treat data hoarding like a disease and surveillance like an insult—so we built a social network and marketplace engineered around zero data retention rather than building a dossier on who you are or where every click leads. While conventional platforms collect names, phone numbers, behavioral profiles, and endless activity histories to monetize or profile users, we refuse to stockpile your personal information or turn your social posts and marketplace activity into a permanent surveillance archive; only what is strictly required for active functionality is handled without building a shadow mirror of your life. Privacy here is a shield for people, never an excuse for harm, so we enforce strict, zero-tolerance boundaries against violent, harmful, and unethical content without turning the platform into a spy machine over innocent members. To keep your presence ephemeral and under your control, messages wipe sixty seconds after being read, dormant accounts automatically self-destruct after fifteen days of inactivity, our Dead Man's Switch executes your pre-set triggers when silence outlasts your threshold, and Pro members can trigger an immediate wipe of local session data and active state on their device simply by entering their duress password—because what isn't hoarded or left behind cannot be mined, leaked, or weaponized against you

We reject the tired internet ritual where "privacy" is just a checkbox approving endless data collection, behavioral profiling, or algorithmic manipulation across your social feed and marketplace trades. Spider Club is built to let you post, discover, and buy or sell without turning your curiosity, purchases, or connections into surveillance bait—and if you follow links outside our walls, their world is theirs, not ours to track you across. We don't hoard your history to feed ad machines or data brokers, because your presence in the club is meant for real exchange, not silent extraction.

​Zero data retention never means zero accountability when it comes to violent, harmful, or unethical content—we draw a hard, uncompromising line against abuse while refusing to turn Spider Club into a mass-surveillance dragnet over innocent people. Every survival control we built—wiping messages sixty seconds after they are read, self-destructing inactive accounts after fifteen days, firing your Dead Man's Switch when silence crosses your threshold, and wiping local session data on the spot when you enter your Pro duress password—exists to keep power with you and leave no lingering trail behind. If we ever update this policy, we update it openly without playing hide-and-seek with your rights

What We Do Not Collect

​We do not require or store real names, phone numbers, home addresses, IP logs, or behavioral tracking profiles. Our social feed and marketplace are designed so you can participate without handing over an identity dossier—and paid plan transactions (Plus and Pro) are handled without storing raw card numbers or sensitive financial records on our servers.

Ephemeral Session & User-Controlled Data

Any handle, post, marketplace listing, or message you create exists strictly for active platform functionality and remains under ephemeral rules: messages wipe 60 seconds after being read, accounts inactive for 15 days self-destruct automatically, Dead Man's Switch triggers follow only your configured instructions, and entering your Pro duress password wipes local session data immediately.
